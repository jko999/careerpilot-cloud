# CareerPilot

CareerPilot is a career assistant designed to simplify the job application process.

## What CareerPilot Does

### CV Optimisation

Analyse your CV against a specific job description and identify how closely they match.

### ATS Readiness

Get an estimated ATS readiness score along with a breakdown of the areas that may need improvement.

### CV Tailoring

Get specific recommendations for adapting your CV to a particular job description, including:

- Current content that could be strengthened
- Suggested replacements
- Relevant keywords that may be missing
- Content that should be reviewed or de-emphasised
- Relevant content that should be retained

### Job Search

Find relevant job opportunities through supported job sources.

### Application Tracking

Keep track of your applications, including the positions you've applied for and their current status.

### Browser Autofill

Use the CareerPilot browser extension to speed up supported application form filling.

## Get Started

Access CareerPilot here:

CareerPilot is designed to help you move from finding a suitable role to tailoring your CV, applying for the position, and tracking your applications from one place.
